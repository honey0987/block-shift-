CHAPTER 2 — THE LEGEND

What is Block Shift?

Every college has its blocks — Tech, Culture, Arts, Sport. For one week, Block Shift knocks down the walls between them and turns the whole campus into a single sprawling Neverland, split into six isles of competition. Teams don't just compete in their department's event — they explore, trade points, and chart their own route across the map.

  • Six isles, six categories, one shared treasure map
  • Open to every year and every department
  • Solo and squad-based events across the week
  • A live leaderboard that updates isle by isle

The Voyage Route

Follow the route from the mainland dock, through the six isles, to the final treasure ceremony on closing night.

CHAPTER 3 — THE SIX ISLES

Pick your isle

Every isle runs its own competition. Tap a badge for the prize and format — then chart which isles your crew will conquer.

Code Kraken

A 24-hour build sprint. Wrestle the Kraken of bugs before the tide comes in.

Teams of 2–4 · 24hr hackathon · Winning crew takes home goodies & certificates.

Art Armada

Paint, sketch, sculpt — a fleet of visual battles across mediums.

Solo entries · On-the-spot themes · Judged by a guest crew of alumni artists · Goodies & certificates for winners.

Music Mutiny

Solo, band, or a cappella crew — take the main deck and steal the show.

5-min slots · Any genre · Live judging on the main-stage deck · Goodies & certificates for winners.

Dance Doubloons

Group choreography battles for the fest's shiniest trophy.

Groups of 6–12 · 4-min routine · Freestyle & choreographed rounds · Goodies & certificates for winners.

Debate Buccaneers

Verbal duels on the plank — argue your case or walk it.

Duos · British parliamentary format · Topics revealed on the day · Goodies & certificates for winners.

Gaming Isles

Bracket-style esports across the fest's favourite titles.

Squads of 4 · Single elimination · Titles announced two weeks prior · Goodies & certificates for winners.
